Delete Your Shadow Inbox Account

Shadow Inbox supports Google account linking. If you want to disconnect a linked Google account and delete associated Shadow Inbox data for that linked account, follow the steps below.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Shadow Inbox.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select your linked Google account.
  4. Choose Disconnect Gmail or remove the linked account.
  5. Confirm the removal request.

What gets deleted

When you remove a linked Google account from Shadow Inbox, we delete the linked OAuth account record and stored OAuth tokens for that Shadow Inbox-linked account, remove the linked feed cache we store for that account, and clear cached inbox data for that account on your device.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or want help with a deletion request, email jleonandersonjr@gmail.com from the Gmail address connected to Shadow Inbox and include the subject Shadow Inbox account deletion request.
