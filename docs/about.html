Shadow Inbox

About

Shadow Inbox is an AI-powered Gmail triage app with arcade-style progression — clear your inbox, level up your fighter, and stay on top of what actually matters.

Built by

Jerome Anderson — independent developer behind Shadow Inbox. The app keeps OpenAI and sensitive credentials on the server so your phone only talks to our secure relay.

GitHub · Contact

What we believe

Email should feel fast and intentional — not like drowning in noise. Shadow Inbox combines server-side AI triage, send-ready drafts in your voice, and a game loop that rewards clearing mail instead of hoarding it.

Beta status

Shadow Inbox is in private Android beta. Features, limits, and availability may change as we learn from early testers. Join the waitlist on the home page.
